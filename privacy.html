Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

DevToolKit is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data.

1. No Data Collection
All tools on DevToolKit run 100% in your browser. We do not collect, store, or transmit any data you enter into our tools. Your data never leaves your device.

2. No Cookies
We do not use cookies or any form of local storage to track your usage.

3. No Analytics
We do not use Google Analytics or any third-party tracking services.

4. No Server-Side Processing
All tool operations are performed client-side using JavaScript. No data is sent to our servers for processing.

5. Third-Party Services
Our website is hosted on Vercel. Vercel may collect standard web server logs (IP addresses, browser type) as part of their hosting infrastructure. Please refer to Vercel's privacy policy for more information.

6. Changes
We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.

7. Contact
If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us.
